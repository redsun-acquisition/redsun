`redsun check` checks each value a device, presenter or view entry gives its constructor against the type and the limits the constructor declares, reporting a value that breaks them as a `value` problem, and decides a key the entry leaves out: `failed` when the session cannot fill it, with a `session` problem at the entry's path when a presenter or view missing it stops the build, and `undecided` when only a provider sharing its type could fill it.
