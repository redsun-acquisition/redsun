`redsun.utils.devices.DevicePath`, `device_path` and `held_by` give where a device sits in its tree, such as `microscope / stage.x`, and whether a plan holds it or a device above it. `AxisInfo.path`, `LightInfo.path`, `Configuration.paths` and `ParamDescription.labels` carry those paths to views, which title boxes, rows and device choices with them.
