The `pyside` extra installs `pyside6-essentials` from 6.10.2 instead of the whole `pyside6`; a project needing a PySide6 add-on module (Charts, 3D, WebEngine...) installs `pyside6` itself. Both Qt extras skip Qt 6.11.0 and 6.11.1, which resize dock widgets wrongly. An environment updated in place after `pyside6` is removed needs `pyside6-essentials` reinstalled.
