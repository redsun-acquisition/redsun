`compute`, a section of the session file and an attribute of a session class: sets the preset (`latency`, `balanced` or `throughput`), the most cores computation may use, the cores kept back and the thread switch interval. A session whose components took an executor limits the native thread pools of `numpy` and other libraries to match until it shuts down. `Session.compute_budget` reports the result as a `redsun.compute.Budget`.
